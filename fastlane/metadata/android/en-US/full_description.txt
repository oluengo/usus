Usus (Latin: practice, habit) lets you log your workouts and habits. Built with calisthenics and home training in mind, it works for any routine of sets and reps.

Free, no accounts, no ads and no Internet connection: everything is stored only on your phone.

<b>What's inside</b>
<ul>
<li><b>Routines</b> with sets, reps or time, per-side exercises, added weight, custom rests, sections, supersets and circuits.</li>
<li><b>Exercise catalogue</b> (optional): a card with what it's for, how to do it, common mistakes, equipment and progression.</li>
<li><b>Guided plan</b>: a few questions and Usus suggests routines at your level and an in-order plan, with deload weeks.</li>
<li><b>Progression</b>: after a session it suggests moving to the next exercise or back to the previous one. You decide.</li>
<li><b>Train</b>: one exercise per screen, full-screen countdown, soft sounds or vibration, and the screen stays on.</li>
<li><b>Diary</b>: sleep, supplements, steps, weight, activity and notes.</li>
<li><b>Progress</b>: consistency, PRs, progress per exercise, left/right balance and habits.</li>
<li><b>Profiles</b> for several people on one phone and optional cycle tracking.</li>
<li><b>Optional, external AI</b>: Usus prepares the text for the AI of your choice to analyse your data or suggest a plan. Usus sends nothing.</li>
<li><b>Share</b> a workout as an image or your routines through a link, with no servers.</li>
</ul>

Backups to a file; in Spanish, Catalan and English. It asks for one permission only: vibration.

It is a logging tool, not medical advice. Built with AI · tested by humans.
